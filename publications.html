✳ 01 / EVIDENCE AND STATUS ✳

What is
established?

My public technical work includes a versioned mathematical preprint with proof notes, exact finite certificates, verification scripts and a frozen PDF. Here I distinguish what can be checked computationally from the analytic claims of the manuscript.

OPEN QUESTION

Exploratory

An open classification or proposed physical test is a research question. A finite witness can demonstrate feasibility in one specified case without settling all cases.

MANUSCRIPT

Internally assessed

A manuscript provides explicit hypotheses and derivations. Finite verification scripts check the supplied instances and certificates, not an unrestricted analytic proof.

EXTERNAL EVIDENCE

Independently assessed

A public DOI fixes a version of the work. Independent proof assessment, reproduced numerical results and journal peer review are separate forms of evidence.

No unpublished draft or repository record is labelled as a journal acceptance without confirmation.

✳ 02 / CURRENT RESEARCH LINES ✳

Where the
results belong.

The public technical-v1 preprint, Nested Regular Covers and Stopping Trees, states four theorem-facing results. These are claims in an archived technical manuscript, not assertions of completed peer review.

THEOREM A / FINITE STABILISATION

Finite-layer bounds

The stated layer bound is Mr = Σd=22r−1(1 + ⌈log₂(d−1)⌉) = O(r log r). See the manuscript for the definition of a nested regular cover.

THEOREMS B–C / SHARPNESS

Thresholds & gaps

In the specified four-edge partial-matching model the transition κℤ(U)=3 to κℤ(2U)=2 requires at least seven states, sharply. A separate integer/real gap is 6 versus 11/2.

THEOREM D / STOPPING TREES

Exact expected cost

The manuscript gives E[Tℓ] = 29/8 − (3/8)·2−ℓ and E[T∞] = 29/8 for its specified stopping-tree construction.

Source: technical-v1 reproducibility repository ↗. Its verification code checks finite certificates and computational claims; it does not formally verify the general analytic proofs.
