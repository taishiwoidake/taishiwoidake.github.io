AN INTUITIVE EXAMPLE

Work with real materials

CAD, machining, conventional and CNC lathes, welding and fabrication teach why an attractive design is not enough: it must work under actual constraints.

THE RESEARCH MODEL

Work with a precise model

I became interested in what it means to prove that an operation is possible, or that no procedure within stated constraints can achieve a goal.

✳   3 / RESEARCH PROFILE   ✳

What can still
be verified?

My central programme, QTRAC, connects operational quantum verification with constrained mathematical computation. I am interested in finite-horizon hidden-memory control, temporal compatibility and information that can be retained before a later test.

CENTRAL PROGRAMME

QTRAC

Sequential quantum access, memory lower bounds, delayed verification and the complexity of choosing actions with hidden state.

FINITE MATHEMATICS

Ximeste–Maluna

Finite-horizon transport, temporal compatibility, constructive bounds and obstructions governed by local operation arity and state size.

RELATED DIRECTIONS

Testing the model

Exact stochastic synthesis, circuit depth, thermal records, directional inverse problems and quantum compiler verification.

✳   4 / RESEARCH PROFILE   ✳

A proof should
survive testing.

I formulate a precise resource or compatibility question, construct small exact instances, search for counterexamples, and develop analytic upper and lower bounds. A reproducible computational certificate can support a finite claim; the general theorem requires its own argument.

→

→

→

SCOPE · Exact computation can verify an individual finite instance; it cannot substitute for a theorem over an unrestricted family.

My public technical-v1 preprint, Nested Regular Covers and Stopping Trees, is archived on Zenodo with an accompanying GitHub reproducibility package. It addresses finite stabilisation, sharp thresholds, integrality gaps and stopping-tree realisation.
