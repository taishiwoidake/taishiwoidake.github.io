From building
to proving.

My route to theoretical questions began with mechanical systems and a high-school artificial-muscle research project.

AN INTUITIVE EXAMPLE

Work with real materials

CAD, machining, conventional and CNC lathes, welding and fabrication teach why an attractive design is not enough: it must work under actual constraints.

THE RESEARCH MODEL

Work with a precise model

I became interested in what it means to prove that an operation is possible, or that no procedure within stated constraints can achieve a goal.

SCOPE · High-school artificial-muscle fabrication is earlier engineering experience, distinct from the present QTRAC research programme.
