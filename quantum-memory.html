Classical versus quantum

Lower bounds,
not slogans.

The research seeks explicit strategies and bounds that prohibit better strategies with less accessible memory.

CONSTRUCTION

An achievable method

Give a precise encoding and decoding protocol, then calculate its performance.

IMPOSSIBILITY

A matched lower bound

Rule out all allowed strategies below a stated memory budget.

COMPARISON

A fair benchmark

Use matching access and question assumptions when contrasting classical and quantum storage.

SCOPE · A numerical separation in a finite model is not a universal quantum-memory theorem.
