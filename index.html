High-school research

My high-school research involved the construction of artificial muscles as a hands-on mechanical-systems project. It was an opportunity to move from an engineering idea to making a physical device.

Design & fabrication

Alongside the project, I gained practical experience in CAD, welding, casting, conventional and CNC lathes, and drill-press work. These are mechanical design and fabrication skills, distinct from my current theoretical work.
