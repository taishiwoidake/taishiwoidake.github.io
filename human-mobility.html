✳   2 / HUMAN MOBILITY   ✳

A crowd is
not an answer.

A busy railway station can reflect work, school, weather, delays and special events. Seeing a crowd does not identify its cause.

INTUITIVE PICTURE

An everyday observation

A crowded platform at 8 a.m. is one observation, not a complete explanation of why everyone is there.

SCIENTIFIC QUESTION

A research question

Define the observed population, collection method, spatial scale and competing hypotheses before estimating patterns or causal effects.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM · Predicting observed movement patterns is not equivalent to establishing causal explanations.
