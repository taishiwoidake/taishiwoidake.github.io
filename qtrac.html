✳ 01 / THE QUESTION ✳

Evidence after
the event.

Past events can leave traces without remaining completely observable. QTRAC formulates an operational verification task instead of assuming the whole microscopic past can be reconstructed.

PAST ALTERNATIVE

What happened?

Fix the earlier event or classical alternative that the future test must distinguish.

ACCESSIBLE INFORMATION

What remains?

A record may spread into degrees of freedom beyond the observer's control.

FUTURE VERIFICATION

What can be tested?

Optimise a decision using only allowed operations at the designated future time.

An event label and a full microscopic history are different targets.

✳ 03 / THE RESOURCE FRONTIER ✳

The order
matters.

Timing changes the resources available to verify the past. The central goal is a precise separation or bound, not an unrestricted reconstruction claim.

ACCESS ORDER

Early or late?

The order of interactions may change which information can be retained.

FINITE MEMORY

How much storage?

The verifier may have access only to a bounded quantum register.

UNKNOWN TIME

When will we test?

A strategy may need to preserve evidence without knowing the eventual deadline.

Any reported separation depends on the full physical model and proof.
